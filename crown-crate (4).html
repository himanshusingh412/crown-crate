Our Story

"I kept sending the same three gifts to every relative, every year. Crown Crate started because gifting deserved better than a default."

— Kartik, Founder, Crown Crate

What began as hand-packed hampers for friends' weddings has grown into a full occasion-led gifting studio — every crate curated, wrapped and sealed in-house, never dropshipped, never generic.
