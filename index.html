About Crown Crate

Gifting deserved better than a default.

“I kept sending the same three gifts to every relative, every year. Crown Crate began the day that stopped feeling like love.”

— Kartik, Founder

What started as hand-packed hampers for friends' weddings is now a full occasion-led gifting studio in Ghaziabad. Every crate is curated, wrapped and sealed in-house. Nothing is dropshipped. Nothing is generic. And every note is read before it's sealed in.
